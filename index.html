Meet Carmen

The story so far

Created with curiosity, compassion, and heart.

Carmen is a lifelong maker who sees possibility everywhere—from watercolor and wire to beads, fiber, resin, clay, and even rescued electronic pieces. Legocita Makes is where those discoveries become meaningful handmade treasures.

Every piece is an invitation to notice beauty, celebrate individuality, and leave someone a little better than you found them.

Made with love, Carmen

How it comes together

What guides the work

A few principles that show up in every piece, no matter the material.

Small, joyful batches

Pieces are made a few at a time, so each one gets real attention rather than being rushed through a production line.

Color with intention

Every palette is chosen deliberately—drawing from nature, mood, and a genuine love of vivid, cheerful combinations.

Materials with character

Wire, beads, resin, fiber, and even rescued odds and ends are treated as building blocks for something new and meaningful.
