Meet the Artist

Hi, I'm Carmen.

I started Legocita Makes because creating has always brought me joy. I love transforming simple materials — watercolor paint, wire, yarn, and reclaimed parts — into meaningful gifts that make people smile.

Every watercolor painting, every bead, every piece of wire is handcrafted right here in Florida. My hope is that each creation leaves someone a little happier than before.
