Meet the maker

Hi, I’m Carmen.

Legocita Makes grew from my love of making things with my hands and my inability to leave a beautiful idea unexplored.

Some days that means bending wire into a tiny flower. Other days it means watercolor, crochet, beads, resin, paper — or something completely unexpected. I love experimenting, combining materials and turning little ideas into pieces that feel happy, personal and handmade.

I don’t want every piece to look exactly alike. The charm is in discovering something a little different.

Made in FloridaSmall batchesHandmade detailsCreative experiments welcome

Why “Makes”?

Because Legocita isn’t limited to one craft. Jewelry may be at the heart of the shop, but watercolor, crochet, paper, resin and new experiments are all part of the story.
