My Story

Hi, I'm Carmen.

I've always loved creating. As a little girl I painted, doodled, and made things with my hands. Today, I still do the same — only now I get to turn that joy into meaningful handmade treasures for other people.

Legocita Makes brings together everything I love: watercolor painting, wire-wrapped jewelry, crochet, and giving new life to reclaimed materials. Every piece is designed, created, and packaged right here in my Florida studio, with a lot of coffee and a grateful heart.

I have a soft spot for the people who take care of others — nurses, therapists, teachers, caregivers — which is why the Healing Hands collection means so much to me. Thank you for supporting a small business and letting me do what I love.

— Carmen

The story behind the name

A Little About Legocita Makes

"Legocita" is a nickname passed down with love — and it felt like the right name for a brand built on handmade, personal, one-at-a-time creations. Legocita Makes isn't a factory or a production line; it's a one-woman studio where every single piece passes through the same two hands, start to finish.

The name is a reminder to keep things small, personal, and made with intention — whether that's a $12 pair of earrings or a custom bridal set. If it says Legocita Makes on it, Carmen made it herself.
