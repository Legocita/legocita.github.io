      <p>I've always loved creating. As a little girl I painted, doodled, and made things with my hands. Today, I still do the same — only now I get to turn that joy into meaningful handmade treasures for other people.</p>
      <p>Legocita Makes brings together everything I love: watercolor painting, wire-wrapped jewelry, crochet, and giving new life to reclaimed materials. Every piece is designed, created, and packaged right here in my Florida studio, with a lot of coffee and a grateful heart.</p>
      <p>I have a soft spot for the people who take care of others — nurses, therapists, teachers, caregivers — which is why the Healing Hands collection means so much to me. Thank you for supporting a small business and letting me do what I love.</p>
